Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

Filtario ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered hiring automation platform operated by QBSoft. We are committed to protecting the personal data of our users, their candidates, and visitors to our website at filtario.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect data in the following ways:

  • Account data: name, email address, company name, billing information when you sign up.
  • Usage data: how you interact with the platform, features used, session duration.
  • Candidate data: resumes, interview responses, skills assessment results uploaded or generated through the platform by our customers (controllers).
  • Communication data: messages you send us via email or support channels.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the Filtario service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmations, invoices, service updates)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Candidate Data

Filtario acts as a data processor for candidate information. Our customers (employers) are the data controllers. We process candidate data solely on the instructions of the employer and do not use it for any other purpose. Candidates have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of their data by contacting the employer who collected it.

5. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Legal bases for processing include: contract performance, legitimate interests, and consent where required. Data is stored on servers located in the European Union. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@filtario.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain account data for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days. Candidate data is retained as configured by the employer (controller). You may request deletion at any time.

7. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third parties including payment processors and cloud infrastructure providers. All third parties are bound by data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We use analytics cookies (with consent) to understand how the platform is used. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest), access controls, and regular security reviews to protect your data.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: privacy@filtario.com
