Hundreds of resumes, no time

A single job post generates 200–500 applications. Your team manually reads each one. Most of their day, gone. The best candidates are buried on page 3.

Scheduling and running first-round calls for 50 candidates takes 2–3 weeks before you even get to real interviews. Recruiters spend 60% of time on admin.
