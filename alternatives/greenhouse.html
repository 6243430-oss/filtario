Common questions

Is Filtario a good alternative to Greenhouse?

Filtario is purpose-built for companies that do high-volume hiring and want AI automation — areas where Greenhouse doesn't go deep. If you're spending more time screening resumes than actually hiring, Filtario addresses that directly. Greenhouse is better for enterprise orgs that need complex approval chains and HRIS integrations.

How much does Filtario cost compared to Greenhouse?

Greenhouse typically costs $6,000–$24,000/year depending on company size, billed annually with a sales process. Filtario starts at €49/month (€588/year) with no contract, no sales call, and a 14-day free trial. At Pro (€149/month), you're at €1,788/year — still a fraction of Greenhouse's cost.

Can I migrate from Greenhouse to Filtario?

Yes. Filtario accepts CSV imports of candidate data from any source including Greenhouse exports. Setup takes 10 minutes and your first AI screening can run the same day.

Does Filtario integrate with the job boards Greenhouse uses?

Filtario aggregates candidates from Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, and other major job boards — the same sources as Greenhouse. All applications land in one ranked pipeline regardless of source.
